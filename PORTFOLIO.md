## Project Title

**Full-Stack E-Commerce Platform with SEO & WhatsApp Ordering — Pinky's Store**

---

## Description

Designed and developed a full-stack e-commerce platform for a beauty, jewelry, and perfume store in Honduras. This is a real, production application actively used by the business owner to manage and sell products. The store operates 100% online, and since its launch, the client has experienced significant sales growth driven by the increased online presence, ease of product browsing, and a frictionless ordering experience via WhatsApp.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 15 (App Router), React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Shadcn/UI (47+ components), Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth, Storage), React Hook Form + Zod.

**Key Features:**

- **Product Catalog & Filtering:** Dynamic catalog with real-time search, category filters (makeup, jewelry, perfumes, accessories), gender filters, product badges (New, Sale, Bestseller, Premium), and stock tracking.

- **WhatsApp Ordering System:** Customers add products to their cart and place orders directly through WhatsApp with a single tap. The system generates a pre-formatted message with all product details, quantities, and totals. This approach eliminated the need for complex payment gateways, reduced friction, and proved to be a key driver of the client's sales growth in the Latin American market.

- **Admin Dashboard:** Secure panel with role-based access control, full product CRUD with image uploads to Supabase Storage, and order management with status tracking (pending, confirmed, shipped, delivered, cancelled). The client manages the entire business autonomously through this dashboard.

- **Advanced SEO:** 5 JSON-LD structured data schemas (Organization, LocalBusiness, WebSite, Breadcrumb, ItemList), dynamic sitemap and robots.txt, Open Graph and Twitter Card metadata, geolocation tags, hreflang for local targeting, and canonical URLs. This SEO strategy was critical to driving organic traffic and strengthening the store's online visibility.

- **Performance & Mobile-First Design:** Next.js Image optimization (AVIF, WebP, lazy loading), aggressive caching (1-year immutable headers), Server Components, code splitting, responsive grid (2→3→4 columns), touch-optimized UI with 44-48px tap targets, and safe area support for notched devices.

- **Security:** HTTP security headers (X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, X-XSS-Protection), restrictive Permissions-Policy, protected admin routes via Next.js middleware, and environment variable separation.

- **UI/UX:** Custom animations (sparkle, slideIn, fadeIn, pop), toast notifications, skeleton loading states, dual typography (Playfair Display + Poppins), and a custom burgundy/pink/gold color palette.

**Impact:** The platform transformed a traditional retail business into a fully online operation. The combination of an intuitive product catalog, one-tap WhatsApp ordering, and strong SEO positioning resulted in measurable sales growth for the client.
